# FAQ

**Is the content on this site final?**

No - the new documentation site is under construction. Pages marked `status: draft` are not yet verified against the product. Always confirm against the product before acting.

**Where do rule pages come from?**

They are generated from `metadata/rules/*.yaml`. See [SQL Audit](en/user-guide/sql-audit/index) and the style guide's [generated-file convention](en/contributing/style-guide).
